Our story

Hearth & Olive began in 2016 as a Saturday supper club in Marisol Vega’s backyard, built around one clay oven she and her father rebuilt brick by brick.

Today the oven sits at the center of our dining room. We cook with olive wood, buy produce from farms within an hour of Riverside, and press our own olive oil each autumn with a neighboring grove.

Everything on the menu is meant to be passed across the table.

What guests notice first

The smell of the oven. A kitchen you can see into. Tables close enough for conversation, and bread that arrives before you’ve finished reading the menu.

Private dining for up to 14 guests is available on request.
